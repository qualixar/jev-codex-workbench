# Code of conduct

We want technical disagreement to be direct, evidence-led, and safe for everyone
participating. Be respectful, critique the work rather than the person, disclose
conflicts of interest, and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, credential sharing,
and deliberate misrepresentation of test or security evidence are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to **varun.pratap.bhardwaj@gmail.com**. Reports will
be reviewed for context and handled as privately as practical. Project maintainers may
edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants whose conduct
puts people or the project at risk.
